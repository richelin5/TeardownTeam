## Known facts and decisions (seeded 2026-11-27 from the research docs)
- Primary user: first-year IB analysts and recruiting candidates who need a trustworthy tear sheet fast.
- v1 scope: US domestic filers (10-K/10-Q), 3 FY + LTM, EV bridge, multiples, credit stats, recent filings, FRED rates strip, a source on every number, one printable page.
- Out of v1: estimates/forward multiples, real-time price (unless decided), IFRS/20-F filers, ownership, credit ratings, peer comps pricing.
- Priority order: correctness and sourcing > company coverage > polish > features.
